## Animations (`<name>-idle.png`, `<name>-face.png`, `<name>-talk.png`)

- **Source:** the front frame (64x64 at 0,0) of each `<name>-sheet.png` above, by the authors credited in its section.
- **Modifications (by BeCarful):** each file is a looping animated PNG made from that one frame by `scripts/tuxemon-idle.py`: the body settles 1–2 px (breathing) or floats, the eyes blink, and some creatures get their own motion (Propellercat's propeller spins and tail sways; Agnidon's flames flicker; Noctalo's and Eaglace's wings flap; Selmatek's floating rocks bob; Moloch's arms sway; Bigfin's tail and sprout sway; Cateye's tail twitches; Hampotamos breathes and blinks; Vamporm's antennae wave; Chillimp's fist bobs; Shybulb waves its leaf arm; Rockitten's ear perks; Budaye's flower-bud tail and Anoleaf's leaf tail sway; Hatchling bobs in its egg, which wobbles; Tumbleworm sways and wiggles its tail). The pixels are moved, and eyelids are painted over the eyes using the sprite's own colors; no new artwork is added. The `-sheet.png` files remain unmodified (the SHA-1s above still apply).
- **Chat faces (`<name>-face.png`, `<name>-talk.png`):** made the same way from the sheet's two 24x24 menu frames. The idle face floats and now and then shows the second menu frame; the talking face alternates the two frames in a speech-like rhythm. Propellercat's talking face instead opens only the mouth, using the open-mouth rows of its second menu frame, and flicks an ear when idle. Shybulb, Rockitten, Budaye, Anoleaf, Hatchling, Tumbleworm and Propellercat faces also blink. Budaye's talking face has a small open mouth drawn in the sprite's own colors (3x3 px), because its second menu frame only moves an eye highlight.
