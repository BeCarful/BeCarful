15 U.S.C. §1015. "State" defined (U.S. Code, 2024 edition)
Source: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title15/html/USCODE-2024-title15-chap20-sec1015.htm
Retrieved: 2026-09-26

As used in this chapter, the term "State" includes the several States, Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, Guam, and the District of Columbia.
