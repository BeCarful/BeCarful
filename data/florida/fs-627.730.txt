Florida Statutes (2026) § 627.730 Florida Motor Vehicle No-Fault Law.
Source: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0600-0699/0627/Sections/0627.730.html
Retrieved: 2026-09-26

Sections 627.730-627.7405 may be cited and known as the “Florida Motor Vehicle No-Fault Law.”
History.—s. 1, ch. 71-252; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 549, 563, ch. 82-243; s. 19, ch. 2003-411; s. 8, ch. 2007-324.
