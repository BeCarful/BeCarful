2281A
FLORIDA NON-CANCELLABLE POLICY
This endorsement is a part of the policy. Except for the
changes this endorsement makes, all other provisions of the
policy remain the same and apply to this endorsement.
The following changes apply until the date specified for the
expiration of this endorsement on the Declarations.
[Editorial]
GENERAL TERMS
The following provision is added:
Notwithstanding any policy provision to the contrary:
a. you may not cancel Liability Coverage
(Bodily Injury and Property Damage),
Property Damage Liability Coverage, or
No-Fault Coverage before the end of the
current policy period.
b. if a notice of cancellation has not been provided by
us within 30 days of the policy effective date, then
we will not cancel Liability Coverage (Bodily Injury
and Property Damage), Property Damage
Liability Coverage, or No-Fault Coverage before
the end of the current policy period.
c. any premium charged for Liability Coverage
(Bodily Injury and Property Damage), Property
Damage Liability Coverage, or No-Fault
Coverage becomes nonrefundable.
d. the policy may not be changed for any reason that
materially increases the risk.
However, if there is a material increase in risk that
requires you to obtain a new policy before the end of
the current policy period, and you obtain the new
policy from us, then we will credit the new policy with
any unearned premium available from this policy.
