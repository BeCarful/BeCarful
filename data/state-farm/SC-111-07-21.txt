LIMITED TRAILER AND PADDOCK COLLISION
COVERAGE
For an additional premium, coverage under PART D – COVERAGE FOR DAMAGE TO YOUR COVERED AUTO
is extended as follows:
We will pay for direct and accidental loss to “your covered auto”, including its “equipment”, shown in the
Declarations or Schedule, less any applicable deductible shown in the Declarations or Schedule, when such loss
is caused by “collision”.
Coverage applies, during the dates indicated in the Declarations or Schedule, while “your covered auto” is:
A. Transported by trailer from one location to another;
B. Loaded and unloaded from the trailer;
C. Driven to and from the trailer to the “paddock” or show display area;
D. On display in the race facility’s “paddock” area or a specified show display area;
E. At the primary garaging or storage location; or
F. Located on a service or repair facility premises.
All other policy provisions apply.
