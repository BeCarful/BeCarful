LOSS PAYABLE ENDORSEMENT
If the Declarations or Schedule shows a loss payee for any covered property, any applicable loss paid or payable
for such listed covered property under this policy will be made to the Named Insured and loss payee shown, as
interests may appear.
If more than one person or entity has an insurable interest in the property covered, we will not be liable in any
one loss:
A. To any person or entity for more than the amount of this person’s or entity’s interest at the time of loss; or
B. For more than the applicable amount of insurance.
We reserve all rights to cancel this policy, as stated in the policy’s cancellation provisions, and that termination
will terminate all future interests of the Named Insured and loss payee. The required advance notice of cancellation
will be made to the Named Insured and the loss payee as shown in the Declarations or Schedule.
All other policy provisions apply.
