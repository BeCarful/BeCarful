ADDITIONAL INSURED
The following is added to PART A – LIABILITY COVERAGE:
ADDITIONAL INSURED
Any liability coverage afforded by this policy for “your covered auto” applies to the additional insured listed in the
Declarations or Schedule, but only as their interest may appear in “your covered auto”. This insurance is subject
to the following additional provisions:
A. We will pay damages for which the additional insured becomes legally responsible only if the damages arise
out of acts or omissions of you or any “family member”.
B. We do not surrender any of our rights of subrogation under this policy.
All other policy provisions apply.
